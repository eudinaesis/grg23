    How does this work?

    A computer tries common passwords and familiar patterns before guessing at random.

    The tester looks for English and German words, names, common passwords, dates and keyboard patterns.

    It estimates how many guesses are needed and gives a score from 0 to 4.

    Online guesses happen at a website. Offline guesses use stolen password data on the attacker's computer.

    The strength calculation runs in your browser. The breach check sends only a short hash prefix, not your password, to Have I Been Pwned.

    Your browser checks the returned results locally. No match does not prove that a password is safe.

    Use invented passwords here. Keep real passwords in a password manager.
