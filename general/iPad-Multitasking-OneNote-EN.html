Three apps at once – touch only

Your goal: OneNote on the left, Safari on the right, Teams floating above them in Slide Over. Use your fingers to control the windows.

1. Get ready

You need iPadOS 26.2 or later, OneNote, Safari and Teams. No keyboard or Pencil is needed.

  1. Hold your iPad in landscape.
  2. Open Settings → Multitasking & Gestures → Windowed Apps.
  3. In OneNote, open the assignment page your teacher has provided. It contains these instructions and space for your work. Start with OneNote filling the whole screen. If it is a small window, tap its Zoom button in the window controls to make it full screen.

Missing an app, the page or a setting? Show your teacher. Do not install updates yourself or bypass school restrictions. No iPad? Practise on a partner's device and swap roles. Say which parts you did together rather than independently.

2. Put OneNote and Safari side by side

  1. Keep OneNote open full screen.
  2. Swipe up a little from the bottom edge until the Dock appears. Do not swipe as far as the centre.
  3. Touch and hold the Safari icon. Drag it from the Dock to the right edge of the screen.
  4. Wait for the side-by-side preview, then let go.
  5. Drag the divider between the windows to the middle.
  6. In Safari, open pnorthup.me/grg23.

Safari is not in the Dock? Open App Library at the far right of the Dock and drag Safari from there.

Check: OneNote left, Safari right. Can you scroll in each app?

3. Add Teams in Slide Over

Slide Over is an extra window that stays above your other windows.

  1. Show the Dock and open Teams.
  2. Touch and hold the window controls at the top of the Teams window.
  3. Tap Enter Slide Over.
  4. Move Teams by dragging its top edge to the side you want. Resize it using the handle in its bottom corner.

No window controls? Swipe down from the top centre of the screen to show the menu bar. Tap the app name there, then touch and hold its window controls.

Check that Teams floats above OneNote and Safari. If necessary, bring those two windows forward and arrange them side by side again. Do not make Teams full screen again.

4. Hide it and bring it back

  1. Swipe Teams off the side of the screen using its top or side edge. An arrow remains at the screen edge.
  2. Check that OneNote and Safari are still side by side.
  3. Swipe inward from that same side edge of the screen to bring Teams back.

Try it: Scroll in OneNote, then Safari. Does Teams stay above them?

Teams only moves like an ordinary window? Check that you chose Enter Slide Over. If the option is missing, ask your teacher.
