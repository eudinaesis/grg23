Follow connections. Explain mechanisms. Judge choices.

Start with Europe’s energy shock, investigate Sri Lanka’s 2022 crisis, and examine the politics of the energy transition. Ask who is exposed, who can act, and when the idea of a polycrisis helps us understand.

Each lesson includes a timed assignment sequence, local readers and activity materials, a printable worksheet, language support, a source trail and public teacher guidance.

Initial teaching draft · 6 October 2026. Historical cases are dated. Hypothetical classroom data are labelled. Assessment arrangements are chosen by the teacher.

This course is publicly accessible by its direct address. Student responses are completed on paper or the teacher’s assignment platform.
