Use these blank templates in class or print them. They are planning aids; filling them in does not replace explaining your reasoning.

1. Crisis map

Write one pressure or event in each box. For every arrow, state the mechanism: How could A affect B? Mark evidence, uncertainty, an alternative explanation, and any connection that could reduce pressure.

Pressure or event AMechanism: how could A affect B?Pressure or event BEvidence / sourceConfidence and uncertaintyAlternative or pressure-reducing link

2. Evidence log

Use one row for each source. Separate what the source claims from the evidence it provides. An author’s argument is not automatically independent evidence for that argument.
