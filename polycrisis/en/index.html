Understanding the polycrisis

Climate, economy and power

16 double lessons · Austrian grades 10–12
CourseSyllabusGlossaryToolkitProjectSourcesTeacher guide

Follow the connections. Test the evidence. Ask who has the power to act.

Three investigations: Europe’s energy shock; Sri Lanka’s 2022 crisis; the politics of the energy transition. We also ask when “polycrisis” is a useful explanation.

English is the main working language. German sources connect international arguments to Austria. Use the German pathway for additional explanations.

A first complete course draft. Your teacher may adjust timings, sources and assessment before teaching.
